## Current focus

### StateArc

**Your work, carried forward.**

I am building StateArc, an open-source, user-owned continuity layer for people and AI agents.

The goal is to carry reliable work context across tools and models while keeping memory:

- **User-owned** — portable and independent of any single AI provider
- **Traceable** — important conclusions retain their sources
- **Correctable** — users can confirm, update, or supersede remembered facts
- **Forgettable** — permissions, retention, and deletion are part of the design
- **Actionable** — agents can use context to help with real work under explicit control

Product decisions, architecture, and implementation will be published here as the project matures.
